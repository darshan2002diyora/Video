# Little Shivaji and the Promise of Swarajya

**Audience:** Ages 3–5  
**Length:** ~10 minutes  
**Format:** 16:9 animated children's story  
**Tone:** Warm, colorful, adventurous, gentle, educational  
**Historical basis:** Inspired by stories from the childhood of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj. Dialogue and scenes are simplified and fictionalized for young children.

## Story

Long ago, in the beautiful hills of Maharashtra, there lived a bright and curious boy named Shivaji.

Shivaji loved the green hills, the tall forts, the horses, and the people who lived around his home. Most of all, he loved listening to his mother, Jijabai, tell stories about brave people who helped others.

One evening, Shivaji sat beside his mother under the warm golden sky.

“Mother,” he asked, “what makes someone truly brave?”

Jijabai smiled. “A brave person is not someone who is never afraid. A brave person uses courage to do what is right and helps people who need them.”

Shivaji thought carefully about her words.

“I want to help people too,” he said.

“I know you will,” said Jijabai.

The next morning, Shivaji walked through the nearby village. He saw a little boy trying to carry a heavy basket.

“Let me help you,” Shivaji said.

Together, they carried the basket to the boy's home.

The boy smiled. “Thank you, Shivaji!”

Shivaji smiled back. “Friends help friends.”

Later, Shivaji noticed that some villagers were worried because a path to their fields had become difficult to use after heavy rain. Shivaji listened to them carefully.

“We can make the path safe again,” he said.

With the villagers working together, stones and branches were moved away. Soon, the path was clear.

Everyone cheered.

Jijabai watched from a distance and smiled proudly.

“You see,” she told Shivaji later, “a leader listens first, then helps people work together.”

As Shivaji grew, he became fascinated by the great forts in the hills. One day, he looked up at a mighty fort standing against the sky.

“What a wonderful place!” he said.

He imagined a land where people could live with dignity, safety, and freedom.

Jijabai taught him an important word: **Swarajya** — the idea of people having self-rule and a land where they could live with dignity.

Shivaji repeated the word softly.

“Swarajya.”

He promised himself that, when he grew up, he would work bravely for his people.

But Jijabai reminded him, “Big dreams begin with small good actions.”

So Shivaji continued learning. He learned to ride horses, understand the hills, listen to people, and care for his friends.

One afternoon, a group of children became lost while playing near the hills. Shivaji heard their worried voices.

“Don't worry!” he called. “Stay together.”

He helped them find a safe path back to the village.

When they returned, their families were relieved and happy.

“You were brave,” one child said.

Shivaji shook his head.

“We were brave together.”

The sun began to set behind the hills. The sky turned orange, pink, and purple.

Shivaji stood beside Jijabai and looked toward the distant fort.

“Mother, I will remember what you taught me,” he said.

Jijabai placed her hand gently on his shoulder.

“Remember this too: true strength is used to protect, not to hurt.”

Shivaji nodded.

Years later, Shivaji Maharaj became a great leader known for his courage, leadership, and care for his people. The little boy who dreamed of helping others had grown into a leader whose story is remembered across India.

And the lesson from his childhood remains simple:

**Be brave. Be kind. Help others. And always do what is right.**

## Ending narration

“Every great journey starts with a small step. And every brave heart can make a difference.”

**THE END**
